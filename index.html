About us.

Hecktic Games is an independent game studio. We build worlds and give experiences.

We understand what a game needs. We play games, listen to the players, and adjust accordingly to make our games worth playing.

We strive to make something interesting, fun, and forever updating. That means no sequels. Why change to the next sequence when all you need is one game.

  • 01Kill the loading screen

    One load from the main menu into the world. Everything after that streams in as you travel. Your time matters.

  • 02Make menus playable

    Menus should be places, not popups. If you can walk there, it's part of the game.

  • 03One world, many games

    Build the world once, fill it with modes. Combat, racing, PvP, sandbox — all in a single continuous space.

  • 04Stay independent

    Small team, big ideas, no publisher telling us to add a battle pass. We make the games we want to play.
